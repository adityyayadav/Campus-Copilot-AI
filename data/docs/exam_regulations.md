# Examination Regulations

## 1. Examination Pattern
Each course is assessed through continuous internal assessment (30%), a mid-semester examination (20%) and an end-semester examination (50%). The mid-semester examination lasts 90 minutes and the end-semester examination lasts 180 minutes. The examination timetable and venues are published on the student portal.

## 2. Eligibility for End-Semester Examinations (Hall Ticket)
A student receives a hall ticket for the end-semester examination only if **all** of the following are met:
1. Attendance in the current term is **at least 75%**.
2. The **Examination Fee** for the term is fully paid.
3. There is no pending disciplinary action that bars the student from examinations.

### 2.1 Attendance Condonation
Students with attendance between **65% and 75%** may apply for condonation to the Head of Department with a valid medical certificate or proof of representing the university at an official event. A condonation fee of **INR 1,000** is payable. Students below 65% attendance are not eligible for condonation and must repeat the course.

## 3. Conduct During Examinations
- Students must be seated 15 minutes before the start time and carry their hall ticket and university ID card.
- No electronic devices, including smart watches, are allowed in the examination hall.
- Any use of unfair means leads to cancellation of the paper and referral to the Disciplinary Committee.

## 4. Results, Revaluation and Re-examination
- Results are declared within 30 days of the last examination.
- A student may apply for **revaluation** within **7 days** of the result declaration, at a fee of **INR 500 per paper**.
- Students who fail a course may appear for the **re-examination** held in the following term, at a fee of **INR 1,200 per course**.
- A student may attempt the re-examination of a course at most **three times**.
