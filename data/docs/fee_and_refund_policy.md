# Fee Payment, Late Fine and Refund Policy

## 1. Fee Components
Fees are charged per term and recorded in the student fee ledger. The components are Tuition Fee, Development Fee, Library Fee, Examination Fee and, for hostel residents, Hostel Fee (which includes the mess charges). The amount due, amount paid, due date and status of each component are visible on the student portal.

## 2. Payment Modes
Fees can be paid online through the student portal (UPI, net banking, debit card) or by demand draft at the Accounts Section. Cash is not accepted. A payment is treated as received on the date it is credited to the university account.

## 3. Instalments
Tuition Fee may be paid in **two equal instalments** if the student submits an instalment request before the original due date. The second instalment is due **60 days** after the first due date. Instalments are not available for Hostel, Library or Examination fees.

## 4. Late Fine
- A late fine of **INR 50 per day** is charged on each fee component that is not fully paid by its due date.
- The fine is calculated from the day after the due date until the date of full payment.
- The late fine is **capped at INR 5,000 per fee component per term**.
- The fine is waived only on medical grounds with a certificate approved by the Dean of Students.

## 5. Consequences of Non-Payment
- Students with an unpaid **Examination Fee** will not receive their hall ticket for the end-semester examination.
- Students with outstanding dues of more than 90 days may have their portal access suspended and hostel allotment cancelled for the next term.
- Degree certificates and transcripts are withheld until all dues are cleared.

## 6. Refund on Withdrawal
Refunds of tuition and development fees on withdrawal from the programme are made as follows, counted from the term start date:

| Withdrawal request submitted | Refund of tuition and development fee |
|---|---|
| Before the term start date | 100% minus INR 1,000 processing charge |
| Within 15 days after term start | 80% |
| 16 to 30 days after term start | 50% |
| More than 30 days after term start | No refund |

The term start date for the 2026-ODD term is **15 July 2026**.

## 7. Hostel Fee Refund
Hostel fees are refunded on a **pro-rata monthly basis** only if the student vacates the hostel for medical reasons or for a university-approved internship of at least three months. In all other cases the hostel fee for the term is non-refundable. The examination and library fees are never refundable.
