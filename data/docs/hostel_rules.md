# Hostel Rules and Regulations

## 1. Allotment
Hostel rooms are allotted every academic year. Priority is given in this order: first-year students, students whose permanent address is more than 100 km from campus, and then by cumulative GPA. Allotment is cancelled if hostel dues are outstanding for more than 90 days.

## 2. Timings
- The hostel gate closes at **10:30 PM**. Late entry requires prior permission from the warden.
- Visitors are allowed only in the visitor lounge between **4:00 PM and 8:00 PM**.
- Mess timings: breakfast 7:30 to 9:00 AM, lunch 12:30 to 2:00 PM, snacks 5:00 to 6:00 PM, dinner 8:00 to 9:30 PM.

## 3. Room Rules
- Cooking appliances, heaters and extension boards above 1000 W are not permitted in rooms.
- Room change requests may be submitted only during the first two weeks of each term.
- Students are responsible for damage to furniture in their room. Damage charges are added to the hostel fee ledger.

## 4. Leave and Vacating
Students leaving the hostel overnight must record the leave in the hostel register. Students vacating the hostel must return the room keys and obtain a no-dues certificate. Hostel fee refund rules are described in the Fee Payment, Late Fine and Refund Policy.

## 5. Discipline
Ragging is a criminal offence and leads to immediate expulsion from the hostel. Alcohol, smoking and narcotics are strictly prohibited. Violations are reported to the Disciplinary Committee and recorded as a disciplinary action on the student's record.

## 6. Contact
Chief Warden's Office, Hostel Block H1. Emergency helpline: extension 2222.
