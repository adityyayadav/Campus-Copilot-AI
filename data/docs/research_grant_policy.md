# Undergraduate Research Grant Policy 2026-27

## 1. Purpose
The Undergraduate Research Grant (URG) programme funds student-led research projects carried out under the supervision of a faculty mentor. Individual grants, their sponsors, amounts and application deadlines are published in the live Research Grants register maintained by the Office of Research and Innovation.

## 2. Grant Tiers and Eligibility
Every grant in the register is assigned a tier. Eligibility is decided by the tier of the grant together with the student's year of study and cumulative GPA (4.0 scale) at the time of application.

### 2.1 Tier A (Advanced Research Grants)
- Open to students in the **third or fourth year** of study.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of **3.5**.
- The project must result in a research paper submission or a working prototype.

### 2.2 Tier B (Applied Project Grants)
- Open to students in the **second, third or fourth year** of study.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of **3.0**.

### 2.3 Tier C (Exploratory Grants)
- Open to students in **any year** of study, including first-year students.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of **2.5**. First-year students without a GPA yet may apply with a faculty recommendation letter.

### 2.4 Department Restrictions
A grant that lists a department is open only to students of that department. A grant with no department listed is interdisciplinary and is open to students of all departments.

### 2.5 General Disqualifications
A student is not eligible for any grant if:
1. the student has an active disciplinary action on record;
2. the student already holds an active (awarded and not yet completed) research grant. A student may hold **only one active grant at a time**;
3. the student's attendance in the current term is below 75%.

## 3. Application Process
1. Identify an open grant in the register whose deadline has not passed.
2. Secure a faculty mentor from the grant's department (any department for interdisciplinary grants).
3. Submit the online application on the Research Portal with: a 2-page project proposal, a budget plan, the mentor's consent form and the latest grade card.
4. Applications close at **11:59 PM IST on the deadline date**. Late applications are not accepted under any circumstances.
5. A student may apply to **at most two grants per academic year**.

## 4. Selection
Applications are reviewed by a committee of three faculty members. Selection is based on novelty (30%), feasibility (30%), student academic record (20%) and expected impact (20%). Results are announced within 30 days after the deadline.

## 5. Disbursement and Reporting
- 60% of the grant amount is released on award and the remaining 40% after the mid-term progress report is approved.
- A final report and a public demo are mandatory within one month of the grant's duration ending.
- Unutilised funds must be returned to the Office of Research and Innovation.
