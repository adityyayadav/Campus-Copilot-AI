# Scholarship Guidelines 2026-27

## 1. Overview
The university offers merit-based, need-based, sports and diversity scholarships. The list of schemes, amounts, number of seats and last dates to apply is maintained in the live Scholarships register. These guidelines define who is eligible and how scholarships are renewed.

## 2. Eligibility by Category

### 2.1 Merit Scholarships (category MERIT)
- Cumulative GPA of **3.6 or above**.
- Open to students in the second year and above.
- Selection is by GPA rank within each department.

### 2.2 Need-Based Scholarships (category NEED_BASED)
- Annual family income below **INR 8,00,000**, supported by an income certificate issued by a competent authority.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of **2.5**.
- The First Generation Learner Grant additionally requires that neither parent holds a university degree.

### 2.3 Women in STEM Scholarship (category WOMEN_IN_STEM)
- Open to women students in any engineering department.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of **3.0**.

### 2.4 Sports Scholarships (category SPORTS)
- Representation of the university at state, national or international level in the last 12 months.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of **2.3**.

## 3. Combining Awards
A student may hold **only one scholarship** at a time. A scholarship **can be held together with a research grant**, since research grants are project funding and not a scholarship.

## 4. Renewal
Scholarships are awarded for one academic year and are renewed only if the student:
1. maintains a cumulative GPA of at least **3.3** (merit) or **2.5** (all other categories);
2. maintains attendance of at least **75%** in each term;
3. has no disciplinary action during the year.

## 5. How to Apply
Apply on the Student Welfare portal before the scheme's last date, with the latest grade card, the income certificate (need-based only) and a statement of purpose of up to 300 words. Incomplete applications are rejected without review.
