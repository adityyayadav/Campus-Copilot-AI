# Student Handbook 2026-27

## 1. Academic Calendar
The academic year has two terms. The ODD term runs from 15 July to 24 December and the EVEN term from 5 January to 25 May. Term codes are written as year and term, for example 2026-ODD.

## 2. Credits and Course Registration
- A student must earn **160 credits** to graduate from a four-year B.Tech programme.
- Students register for courses on the portal during the first week of each term. Core courses are registered automatically; electives must be chosen by the student.
- An elective runs only if at least 10 students register. Registration closes when the course capacity is reached.
- A student may take at most **26 credits** in a term.

## 3. Attendance
Minimum attendance of **75%** is required in every term. Attendance below this limit makes the student ineligible for end-semester examinations unless condoned as per the Examination Regulations. Attendance also affects scholarship renewal and research grant eligibility.

## 4. GPA and Academic Standing
- GPA is calculated on a 4.0 scale as the credit-weighted average of grade points. The grade point table is given in the Examination Regulations.
- **Dean's List:** students with a term GPA of 3.75 or above are placed on the Dean's List.
- **Academic Probation:** students whose cumulative GPA falls below **2.0** are placed on academic probation, may register for at most 18 credits and must meet an academic advisor every month.

## 5. Minors and Honours
Students with a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or above at the end of the second year may register for a Minor in another department or for Honours in their own department, requiring an additional 18 credits.

## 6. Code of Conduct
Students must carry their ID card on campus at all times. Plagiarism in assignments or projects leads to a zero grade for the assessment and, on repetition, referral to the Disciplinary Committee. A disciplinary action, once recorded, remains active for one academic year.

## 7. Student Support
- Academic advising: every student is assigned a faculty advisor from their department.
- Counselling centre: Student Activity Centre, open 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Grievances can be submitted on the Student Grievance portal and are answered within 7 working days.
