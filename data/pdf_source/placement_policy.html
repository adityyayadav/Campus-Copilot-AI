Training and Placement Policy 2026-27

Policy No. TPO/2026/04 · Effective from 1 July 2026 · Approved by the Academic Council

1. Purpose

This policy sets out who may register for campus placements and internships, how offers are accepted, and the conduct expected from students during the placement season. The Training and Placement Office (TPO) maintains the list of registered students and visiting companies.

2. Eligibility for Campus Placements

A student may register for final placements only if all of the following conditions are met at the time of registration:

  • The student is in the fourth (final) year of study.
  • The student has a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75 on the 4.0 scale.
  • The student has no course with a failing grade (F) that has not yet been cleared.
  • Attendance in the current term is at least 75 percent.
  • The student has no active disciplinary action on record.
  • All fee dues for the current term are fully paid. Students with any outstanding fee balance must clear their dues before registration is approved.

3. Eligibility for Internships

Students in the third year of study may register for summer internships offered through the TPO. The minimum cumulative GPA for internship registration is 2.5 on the 4.0 scale. Third-year students are not eligible for final placement offers.

An internship of at least eight weeks, completed with a satisfactory report from the host organisation, earns 4 academic credits.

4. Offer Acceptance Rules

The university follows a one-student-one-offer principle so that opportunities are shared fairly.

Offer categoryAnnual package (CTC)Rule after accepting
RegularBelow INR 8 lakhMay apply to one Dream company only

An offer once accepted cannot be declined in favour of a later offer in the same category.

5. Conduct During the Placement Season

  • Students must report 30 minutes before the scheduled start of any test or interview.
  • A student who registers for a company and is absent from its selection process without prior written permission is debarred from the next two placement drives.
  • Misrepresentation of grades or experience in a resume leads to removal from the placement process for the academic year and referral to the Disciplinary Committee.

6. Documents Required for Registration

  • Resume in the TPO standard format.
  • Latest grade card issued by the Controller of Examinations.
  • University identity card.
  • No-dues certificate from the Accounts Section.

7. Placement Season Calendar

Registration for the 2026-27 placement season opens on 1 August 2026 and closes on 31 August 2026. Pre-placement talks run from September, and company drives continue until 31 March 2027. Internship registration for third-year students opens on 1 December 2026.
